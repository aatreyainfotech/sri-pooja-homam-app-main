1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register or use the app:

  • Name, mobile number, and email address (for account creation and OTP verification)
  • Booking details: pooja/homam name, scheduled date, devotee name, gotra, nakshatra
  • Payment information (processed securely via Razorpay; we do not store card/UPI details)
  • Device push notification tokens (for booking updates and reminders)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm pooja/homam bookings
  • To send OTP for account verification via WhatsApp
  • To send booking confirmations and live stream notifications
  • To provide customer support

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Razorpay – for payment processing
  • Meta (WhatsApp Business API) – for OTP and notifications
  • Microsoft Azure – for secure cloud hosting and database storage
  • Agora – for live streaming services

4. Data Retention

We retain your account and booking data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting us at support@aatreya.org.

5. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored as hashed values. We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting support@aatreya.org.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
